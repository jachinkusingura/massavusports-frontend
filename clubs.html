How we keep club information accurate

Club names, crests and fixtures are reviewed by our Kampala data desk against official competition information and club announcements. Clubs sometimes rebrand, move stadiums or change sponsors during a season, so if you represent a club and want your details or crest updated, send us a message on the Contact page.

Club names are used here only to identify teams in the fixtures and results we publish. MassavuSports is an independent information site and is not affiliated with any club, FUFA, or any league body. See our Disclaimer.
