Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 4, 2026

At MassavuSports (https://massavusports.com), protecting the privacy of our visitors is one of our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that are collected and recorded by MassavuSports and how we use and safeguard that information.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit MassavuSports, you may browse match fixtures, live scores, league standings, and editorial articles without registering for an account. If you choose to contact us directly via our Contact Us page or email, we may receive your name, email address, and the contents of your message strictly for responding to your inquiry.

2. Cookies, Local Storage & Web Beacons

Like standard digital publications, MassavuSports uses browser cookies and HTML5 localStorage to store visitor preferences—such as your pinned Ugandan football clubs, selected league filters, and cached match schedules—to optimize page load speed and customize your experience.

3. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is a third-party vendor on our website. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve advertisements to our site visitors based upon their visit to massavusports.com and other sites on the internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising and the use of DART cookies by visiting the official Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads or managing Ads Settings at https://myadcenter.google.com/.

4. Log Files & Anonymous Analytics

MassavuSports follows a standard procedure of using log files and anonymous visitor analytics. The information collected includes anonymized page views, browser type, device category (Mobile, Tablet, Desktop), date/time stamp, and referring pages. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, and understanding which Ugandan football leagues and clubs our readers follow most closely.

5. Optional Web Push Notifications

If you explicitly opt in to receive push notifications on MassavuSports, a standard browser Web Push subscription token is generated. This allows us to send match score alerts and fixture reminders. Push notifications are 100% opt-in and can be disabled at any time through your browser settings.

6. GDPR & CCPA Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data you have submitted to us, as well as the right to object to or restrict processing. To exercise any of these rights, please contact our editorial and data team at jachinkusingura60@gmail.com.

7. Children's Information

MassavuSports does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child provided this kind of information on our website, please contact us immediately and we will promptly remove such information from our records.
