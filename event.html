UPL Matchday 10 Roundup: BUL FC & NEC FC Tighten Grip at the Summit as Title Race Heats Up

MassavuSports Editorial Desk 2026-11-04 5 min read

The 2026/27 Uganda Premier League campaign has reached a thrilling inflection point at Matchday 10, with perennial contenders and ambitious challengers trading blows at the top of the table. Tuesday's opening fixtures delivered high drama across three venues as BUL FC, NEC FC, and Police FC all took to the pitch in crucial first-round encounters.

In Jinja, BUL FC delivered a statement performance by edging record champions SC Villa 2-1 in front of a packed crowd. Abbey Kikomeko's side combined disciplined midfield pressing with clinical transition play, neutralizing Villa's wide attackers early in the first half. Despite a late surge from the Jogoos—who had entered the fixture in buoyant scoring form following victories over Mbarara City and Lugazi FC—BUL held firm in stoppage time to claim all three points and cement their status as genuine title favourites.

Simultaneously in Lugogo, NEC FC continued their remarkable consistency with a hard-fought 1-0 victory over UPDF FC. Having already dismantled Ntugasaze FC 5-1 on Matchday 8 and edging Blacks Power 1-0 away on Matchday 9, NEC showcased the defensive resilience that has defined their campaign. Their backline has now kept five clean sheets in ten outings, making them one of the toughest sides to break down in Ugandan top-flight football.

At the Kavumba Recreation Ground, Police FC and Kitara FC shared the spoils in an entertaining 1-1 draw. Both sides created clear-cut opportunities in the second period, with Kitara looking to build on their recent 1-0 victory over UPDF FC while Police sought back-to-back wins after overcoming Kataka FC 2-1 last Friday.

Attention now shifts to Wednesday and Thursday's blockbuster fixtures: KCCA FC host Maroons FC in a classic Kampala-area showdown, Kataka FC welcome URA FC in Mbale, and defending heavyweights Vipers SC prepare to host a resilient Lugazi FC side under the floodlights at St. Mary's Stadium, Kitende.
