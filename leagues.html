The structure of Ugandan league football

Domestic club football in Uganda is organised by the Federation of Uganda Football Associations (FUFA). The professional pyramid is led by the Uganda Premier League, the top division, with the FUFA Big League as the second tier directly beneath it. Clubs that finish at the bottom of the top division risk dropping down a level, while the strongest Big League sides compete for the chance to move up, which is what makes both ends of both tables meaningful to supporters.

Below these two divisions sit regional and community leagues where many of today's well-known clubs began. MassavuSports currently concentrates on the top two tiers so that every fixture, result and table we publish can be checked carefully against official sources.

Uganda Premier League (UPL)

The UPL is the highest level of club football in the country. Clubs play each other home and away over the course of the season, and the club with the most points at the end is crowned champion. Champions and leading finishers typically earn the right to represent Uganda in continental club competitions run by the Confederation of African Football (CAF), subject to the regulations in force for that year.

Some of the best-known names you will see in our fixtures include Vipers SC, KCCA FC, SC Villa, URA FC, Express FC and Police FC, alongside newer sides making their mark in the division. See the full list on our Clubs directory.

What to watch for in a UPL season

  • The title race: the gap between the top clubs often changes quickly, so a single postponed fixture can decide who leads the table.
  • The continental places: finishing high matters beyond bragging rights because of qualification for CAF competitions.
  • The relegation battle: clubs at the foot of the table fight to avoid dropping to the Big League.

FUFA Big League

The FUFA Big League is the second division of Ugandan football. It is split into groups so that clubs from the same part of the country play each other more regularly, which keeps travel costs manageable for clubs and brings top-flight-level football to towns across the country, including the West Nile, Western, Central and Eastern regions.

Because the Big League is organised in groups, standings are shown per group rather than as one national table. Winning a group is the first step on the path to promotion, so every result in the Big League can carry as much weight for its clubs as a title decider does in the top division.

Why the Big League matters

  • It is the main pathway for ambitious regional clubs to reach the Premier League.
  • It develops young players who later move up to UPL clubs.
  • It keeps professional football accessible to communities far from Kampala.

How points and standings work

League tables on MassavuSports follow the standard football points system:

Reading the table columns

  • P (Played): matches completed so far.
  • W / D / L: wins, draws and losses.
  • GF / GA: goals scored (for) and conceded (against).
  • GD (Goal difference): goals for minus goals against.
  • Pts (Points): total points, calculated as (wins × 3) + draws.

Clubs are ranked by points first. When two or more clubs are level, tie-breakers such as goal difference and goals scored are applied as laid out in the competition regulations. Official rules are always published by FUFA; if a table here ever differs from an official ruling, the official ruling takes priority and we will correct our data.

Match statuses explained

StatusMeaning
Scheduled / UpcomingThe match has not started yet. Kick-off time is shown in East Africa Time (UTC+3).
LiveThe match is in progress. Scores update as results are verified.
FT (Full Time)The match has finished and the result counts in the standings.
PostponedThe match was called off before kick-off and will be replayed on a date to be confirmed. It does not count in the standings until played.

Frequently asked questions

How many points is a win worth?

A win earns 3 points, a draw 1 point and a defeat 0 points.

How are teams ranked when they are level on points?

Points come first. Tie-breakers such as goal difference and goals scored are then applied according to the competition regulations published by FUFA.

What does "Postponed" mean?

The fixture was called off before kick-off and will be replayed later. It does not count in the standings until it has been played.

Which time zone are kick-off times shown in?

All times are East Africa Time (EAT, UTC+3), the local time in Kampala.
