About MassavuSports

Uganda's dedicated football data platform — delivering accurate fixtures, results, standings, and lineups for local leagues that matter to Ugandan fans.

Our Mission

MassavuSports was built to bridge the gap between fans and the pitch. For too long, Uganda's local football ecosystem lacked a central, reliable hub where supporters could track their teams in real time. We set out to change that.

Our mission is simple: provide authentic, up-to-date football data for Ugandan leagues — from the top-flight Uganda Premier League down to passionate community competitions like the Ntare, Chaapa, and Kitunga leagues. Every fixture, result, and standing you see on this platform is manually verified by our editorial team before it goes live.

How We Source Our Data

Transparency matters to us. Here is exactly how match data gets onto MassavuSports:

  • Official FUFA communications — Fixture schedules, referee assignments, and competition rulings are sourced directly from FUFA's official channels and matchday programmes.
  • On-the-ground reporting — Our team of regional correspondents attend or monitor matches directly for community league results (Ntare, Chaapa, Kitunga), where no official digital feed exists.
  • Editorial verification — Every score and fixture entered through our admin system is cross-checked against at least one independent source before publication.
  • Supabase cloud sync — Data is stored in a secure, cloud-backed database ensuring consistency across all devices and sessions in real time.
  • Correction policy — If we publish an incorrect score, we correct it within 24 hours and note the amendment. Spotted an error? Contact us.

Editorial Independence

MassavuSports is independently operated and has no commercial ties to any football club, sponsor, or governing body. Our only obligation is to Ugandan football fans who rely on this platform for accurate information.

We display Google AdSense advertisements to cover hosting and operational costs. Advertising relationships have zero influence on our match reporting, standings calculations, or editorial decisions.

For media inquiries, data correction requests, or partnership proposals, please reach out via our Contact page.
